## Possible errors in the app's guides (for Zac and the app's checks; the episodes follow the guides)
- Contract WS07: the guide puts all three restraint-of-trade requirements on the party relying on the restraint; in law
  the party attacking it proves it is contrary to the public interest (Herbert Morris v Saxelby). The episode now lists
  the three requirements without saying who proves the third.
- Contract WS07: "only" non est factum escapes the signature rule; L'Estrange v Graucob is itself subject to fraud and
  misrepresentation (Curtis v Chemical Cleaning). The episode keeps the guide's wording.
- WAE WS07: an outright cash gift made at least 7 years before the donor first occupies the property doesn't meet the
  pre-owned assets contribution condition (FA 2004 Sch 15 para 10(2)(c)); for intangibles the deduction is the income
  tax or CGT already charged under the settlor-interested rules, not payments for use (para 9); for land and chattels
  only payments the person is legally obliged to make are deducted.
- WAE WS07: gifts with reservation: FA 1986 s 102(5) protects more exempt gifts than the four the guide lists (e.g.
  gifts to political parties or for national purposes); the care exception needs an "unforeseen" change of
  circumstances (FA 1986 Sch 20 para 6(1)(b)), not "unforeseeable".
