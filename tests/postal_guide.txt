WORKSHOP: Contract WS01 test guide
OVERVIEW
Acceptance must be communicated to the offeror. The postal rule is an exception: acceptance by post takes effect when posted, even if the letter is lost. Adams v Lindsell. Household Fire and Carriage Accident Insurance Co v Grant. Withdrawal of an offer only takes effect when it reaches the offeree. Instantaneous communication such as phone or email takes effect on receipt. The postal rule can be excluded by the offer's wording.
